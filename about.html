I'm Numan — a product designer who grew up in Dhaka 🇧🇩 and now lives in Oslo 🇳🇴.

I work end-to-end — research, UX, UI, and the business thinking behind them.

Right now I'm building MistetFunnet, Norway's first AI-assisted lost-and-found infrastructure. I founded it after losing something in Ålesund and realising the country still runs on Facebook groups for this. Pre-seed funded, first B2B partner signed, kommune pipeline next.

Before that I designed campaigns for Daraz (Alibaba Group), built UI systems for Norwegian and EU clients, and spent two years animating explainers in Dhaka. I'm finishing a Master's at Høgskulen i Volda in 2026. My thesis applies human-centered design to rights-advocacy work in Bangladesh.

I work best in small teams that ship.
